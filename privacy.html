Privacy Policy

The Royal Treasury Discord bot · Effective October 16, 2026

Summary

Royal Treasury stores Discord identifiers and game records needed to operate its fictional economy. This includes balances, progress, transactions, and certain text submitted through its features. It does not require your real name, email address, or payment details to play, and does not use game data for advertising.

What the bot stores

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, and relevant channel or message IDs used to maintain accounts, permissions, and interactions.
  • Gameplay records: balances, loans and debts, inventory, companions, estates, investments, guilds, achievements, statistics, cooldowns, and event participation.
  • Transaction records: in-game ledger entries and related details used to keep balances accurate, resolve disputes, and investigate errors.
  • Information you submit through the bot: guild names, custom names, transfer memos, and other optional game inputs. Avoid including sensitive personal information in these fields.
  • Operational information: configuration, relevant interaction references, and error information needed to run and troubleshoot the service.

What the bot does not store

Royal Treasury does not ask players to provide passwords, payment-card information, or real names. Its normal gameplay does not require the contents of unrelated Discord conversations. Some message text may be processed to recognize enabled text commands, and text you deliberately submit to game features may be retained as described above.

Discord and the hosting service independently process technical and account information under their own policies. This policy does not describe or control their separate practices.

How the data is used

  • To run the bot's games and economy, show balances and leaderboards, and post game results and the weekly Gazette in the server.
  • To send you direct messages about your own game activity, such as a guild contest that needs your response. You can turn off direct messages from server members in your Discord privacy settings.
  • To let server staff review transactions and correct errors.

Sharing

  • Royal Treasury does not sell gameplay information or share it with advertisers for targeted advertising.
  • Game information may be visible to members through commands, profiles, leaderboards, transactions, and the Gazette, depending on the feature used.
  • Discord and the bot's hosting provider necessarily process information to deliver and operate the service. Administrative settings and aggregate economy figures may be transmitted to the operator's dashboard.
  • Information may also be disclosed if legally required or necessary to investigate security incidents or misuse.

Storage and security

Gameplay records are kept in a database operated on the bot's hosting infrastructure. Authorized bot operators and hosting services may have access where necessary to maintain the service. Reasonable access controls are used, but no system can guarantee perfect security.

Account and transaction records are generally retained while needed to operate the game, preserve shared balances, investigate misuse, or resolve disputes. Records may remain in operational backups for a limited time where backups are maintained. A fixed automatic deletion schedule is not currently offered.

Deleting your data

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your Discord user ID by contacting the bot's operator in the Kingdom of Asteria Discord server. The operator may need to verify that you control the account.

Requests are handled manually. Deleting account data can remove virtual currency, items, and progress. Some transaction records involving other players may need to be retained in limited or de-identified form to preserve financial history, comply with legal obligations, or prevent abuse. The operator will explain any applicable limitation when responding to a request.

Children

The bot is intended only for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact the bot's operator through the Kingdom of Asteria Discord server and ask for a private support or privacy request. Do not post personal information in a public channel. See also the Terms of Service.
